Preformed Defects

Effect of flat surface with the sharp corner


This simulation demonstrates the effects of flat surface defects that occur during forging operations with preformed billets. Flat surface defects can result from improper die design, inadequate material flow, or insufficient deformation in certain regions of the workpiece. These defects affect the dimensional accuracy and surface finish of the forged component.
